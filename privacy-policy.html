SpecialNStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains how SpecialNStudio ("we", "us") handles information on this website and in SpecialNStudio Pin Scheduler, our internal app that connects to the Pinterest API.

Who we are

SpecialNStudio is a small design studio selling digital products on Etsy (etsy.com/shop/SpecialNStudio). Contact: specialnstudio@gmail.com.

SpecialNStudio Pin Scheduler

SpecialNStudio Pin Scheduler is a private tool used only by the owner of SpecialNStudio. It is not offered to other people and no one else can sign in to it. It publishes Pins of our own Etsy product photos to our own Pinterest business account.

  • What the app accesses on Pinterest: only our own account. With the permissions boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write and user_accounts:read it lists and creates our boards, creates Pins and reads our account name to confirm the right account is connected.
  • What the app stores: the Pinterest access and refresh tokens of our own account, kept encrypted, and a list of which product photos have been pinned (photo address, Pin title, description, board and Pin ID).
  • What the app does not do: it does not read, collect, store, sell or share any information about other Pinterest users. It does not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than publishing our own Pins.
  • Product data: Pin content comes from our own public Etsy listings (titles, descriptions, tags and photos).

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking and does not collect personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in its own privacy statement.

Purchases

All purchases happen on Etsy. Order information is handled under Etsy's Privacy Policy. We only use the order details Etsy provides to deliver your purchase and to answer your messages, and we never sell them.

Data retention and deletion

Pinterest tokens are kept only as long as the app is connected. We can disconnect the app at any time in our Pinterest settings, after which the tokens stop working and are deleted. To ask a question about your data or to request deletion, email specialnstudio@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
